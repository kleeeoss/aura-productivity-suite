# AURA v1.1.1 — The Multiverse Visual Engine & Architectural Design System

Welcome to **AURA v1.1.1**! This milestone release introduces the **Multiverse Visual Engine**, a ground-up transformation of AURA's visual and tactile experience.

Rather than treating themes as simple color swaps or dark/light mode toggles, AURA 1.1.1 reimagines personalization as **complete architectural worldbuilding**. Every theme is an authentic, cohesive environment governed across **10 orthogonal design dimensions** and an expressive **4-tier typography hierarchy**.

---

## 🌌 The Multiverse Visual Engine (10 Orthogonal Dimensions)

Every visual style in AURA 1.1.1 is defined across 10 disciplined architectural parameters:

1. **Theme World Concept & Atmosphere**: The overarching philosophy, artistic lineage, and mood (e.g., aerospace HUD, VT220 retro console, CAD drafting desk, or antique library).
2. **4-Tier Typography Hierarchy**: Purpose-built font roles spanning Display, Body, Data/Mono, and Accent/Kicker typography tailored to each theme's atmosphere.
3. **Component Silhouette Geometry**: Shape geometry ranging from razor-sharp 0px chamfers to aerospace 14px rounded glass and organic asymmetric pebble curves (`18px 6px 18px 6px`).
4. **Borders & Linework**: Structural strokes spanning subtle 1px aerospace hairlines, 1px cyan dashed CAD drafting lines, 2px stepped pixel borders, to high-impact 3px neo-brutalist solid pitch-black borders.
5. **Shadows & Depth**: Optical elevation including refractive frosted acrylic blurs, CRT phosphor bloom, physical 4px offset paper shadows (`4px 4px 0px`), flat unshadowed linen, and pure pitch-black OLED depth.
6. **Surfaces & Procedural Textures**: Material backdrops featuring CRT raster scanlines, millimeter coordinate grids, warm linen broadsheet grain, Game Boy dot matrix textures, and stealth obsidian surfaces.
7. **Spacing & Spatial Density**: Ergonomic calibration tuned for each workflow—from high-density telemetry cockpits to spacious literary broadsheets.
8. **Motion Dynamics & Physics**: Custom animation timing curves: instant 0ms VT220 step-end transitions, 60ms 2-frame retro arcade steps, snappy 100ms brutalist snaps, to graceful 400ms broadsheet easing.
9. **Interaction Feedback**: Tactile visual responses including physical button compression (`translate(2px, 2px)`), phosphor bloom, laser illumination, and organic floating lifts.
10. **Telemetry & Progress Visualization**: Distinct geometric progress meters tailored to each world—Aerospace HUD arcs, VT220 ASCII brackets (`[████░░] 60%`), Neo-Brutalist segmented blocks, Blueprint calipers (`CAL-065mm`), 8-Bit heart/XP bars (`HP: [♥♥♥♡♡] 60%`), and OLED laser rings.

## 🪐 8 Canonical Theme Worlds

AURA 1.1.1 ships with 8 hand-crafted, production-tested canonical theme worlds:

* **🛸 Translucent Cockpit (Aerospace Flight HUD)**:
  Focused, precise, modern ambient glassmorphism with aerospace telemetry highlights, Space Grotesk display headers, and frosted acrylic depth.
* **💻 Terminal / Cyber-CLI 1984 (VT220 Distraction-Free Console)**:
  Phosphor CRT amber/green glow, instant 0ms response, authentic ASCII linework, scanlines, and pure monospaced focus.
* **🎨 Neo-Brutalist Studio (High-Contrast Tactile Poster)**:
  Hard 3px pitch-black borders, physical 4px offset drop-shadows, Archivo Black display typography, vibrant saturated paper backgrounds, and bold tactile confidence.
* **📜 Editorial Broadsheet (Antique Literary Sanctuary)**:
  Delicate warm ink rules, cream linen base, graceful Fraunces & Merriweather serif typography, and calm, distraction-free broadsheet reading flow.
* **📐 Technical Blueprint (CAD Draftsman Workstation)**:
  Millimeter coordinate grids, cyan dashed borders, registration crosshairs, Space Mono typography, and engineering caliper telemetry (`CAL-065mm`).
* **🕹️ 8-Bit Arcade (Microcomputer Nostalgia)**:
  Authentic 4-shade Game Boy green palette, dot matrix textures, stepped pixel borders, 2-tone bevel buttons, and pixelated hearts/XP health bars (`HP: [♥♥♥♡♡]`).
* **🖤 Obsidian Monolith (Neo-Noir OLED Cockpit)**:
  Stealth, zero-eye-strain nocturnal cockpit with pure `#000000` OLED black, whisper-quiet laser edges, and subtle violet illumination.
* **🍵 Zen Botanical (Wabi-Sabi Meditative Sanctuary)**:
  Organic earthy matcha and stone tones, asymmetric pebble geometry (`border-radius: 18px 6px`), tranquil water basin ripple telemetry, and soothing Plus Jakarta Sans typography.

## ⏱️ Connected Focus & Timer Experiences

* **Theme-Native Focus Telemetry**: The Pomodoro, Short Break, and Long Break focus timers adapt their visual presentation dynamically to your chosen theme world. Cyber-CLI renders a real-time ASCII bracket bar, Neo-Brutalist renders segmented tactile blocks, 8-Bit Arcade renders retro health hearts, and Technical Blueprint displays millimeter caliper gauges.
* **Specimen Matrix in Settings**: Inspect the live 10-dimension specimen board directly inside the Settings view to preview font pairings, border metrics, tactile button states, and progress telemetry before switching.
* **Instant Command Palette Switching**: Search and toggle any of the 8 canonical worlds seamlessly with `Ctrl+K`.
